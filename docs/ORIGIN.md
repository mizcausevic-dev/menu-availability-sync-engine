# Origin

Menu Availability Sync Engine is part of the Kinetic Gain atlas expansion into Food / Restaurant Tech.

The repo exists to make a specific commercial and operator story visible:

- menu availability is not just CMS content
- marketplace sync and modifier drift create real revenue risk
- stale cache layers break loyalty and app trust
- search-ordering mismatches hurt local discovery and launch posture

This surface turns those operational problems into one buyer-readable control plane with named owners, required evidence, and release timing.
